# Artwork file requirements

Please send print-ready artwork as **PDF/X-4** or vector **AI/EPS** files. Requirements:

- Use our dieline template for your product (download it from the product page).
- Colour mode **CMYK**; Pantone spot colours are available for orders above 2,000 units.
- Images at least **300 dpi** at final size.
- Convert text to outlines or embed fonts.
- Add **3 mm bleed** on all sides.

We do not accept Word, PowerPoint or Canva share links as print files. Our prepress team checks every file for free and tells you within one business day if something needs fixing.
