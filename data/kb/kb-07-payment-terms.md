# Payment methods and terms

New customers pay **upfront** by card, iDEAL, SEPA bank transfer or PayPal before production starts.

Business customers with three or more completed orders can apply for **invoice payment with 30-day terms** (net 30), subject to a credit check.

Invoices are issued in EUR. We charge Dutch VAT (21%) to customers in the Netherlands and to EU consumers; EU businesses with a valid VAT number are invoiced with reverse charge (0% VAT). Customers outside the EU are invoiced without VAT.
