# Minimum order quantities

Kestrel Print Co. produces custom printed packaging for brands. The minimum order quantity (MOQ) depends on the product:

- Mailer boxes: 250 units per design.
- Folding cartons (retail boxes): 500 units per design.
- Printed paper bags: 500 units per design.
- Stickers and labels: 1,000 units per design (any mix of sizes on one roll counts as one design).
- Tissue paper: 1,000 sheets per design.

Samples below the MOQ are available as a paid sample pack (see "Samples and sample packs"). Reorders of an existing design have the same MOQ.
