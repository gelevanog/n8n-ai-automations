# Pricing and volume discounts

Prices depend on the product, size, material, print colours and quantity. The online configurator on our website gives an instant price for standard sizes.

Volume discounts are applied automatically:

- 1,000-4,999 units: 5% off the list price
- 5,000-9,999 units: 10% off
- 10,000 units and more: 15% off

All prices are in EUR and exclude VAT. For custom sizes, special finishes (foil, embossing, soft-touch) or orders above 25,000 units, ask for a quote: our sales team replies within one business day.
