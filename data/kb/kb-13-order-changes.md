# Changing or cancelling an order

You can change quantities, artwork or the delivery address **free of charge until you approve the proof**.

After proof approval production is scheduled, and the order can only be cancelled if printing has not started: we then charge a 15% cancellation fee to cover prepress and material. Once printing has started an order cannot be cancelled.

To change the delivery address after shipping, contact support with your tracking number; the carrier may charge a redirection fee.
