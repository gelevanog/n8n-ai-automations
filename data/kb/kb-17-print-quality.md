# Print methods and colours

We print digitally for runs up to 2,000 units and offset (litho) for larger runs.

- Digital printing: full colour CMYK, no plate costs, ideal for small runs and multiple designs.
- Offset printing: full colour CMYK plus up to two Pantone spot colours, the best colour consistency for large runs.
- White ink is available on kraft board.

Inside printing (on the inside of a mailer box) is available for an extra 20% of the box price.
