# Materials and sustainability

Our mailer boxes and cartons are made from **FSC-certified** corrugated board or folding boxboard. Standard boxes contain at least 70% recycled fibre.

We print with **water-based and soy-based inks**. All our paper products are recyclable with paper waste, and our compostable mailer bags are certified to EN 13432.

We do not offer plastic bubble wrap or plastic-laminated finishes; soft-touch finish uses a recyclable aqueous coating.
