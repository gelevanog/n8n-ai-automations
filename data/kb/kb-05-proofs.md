# Digital proofs and approval

After you upload artwork we send a **digital proof (PDF)** within one business day. Production starts only after you approve the proof in your account.

- Up to **two rounds of changes** to the proof are free; further rounds cost EUR 25 each.
- A **physical pre-production proof** (one printed and assembled unit) costs EUR 75 and adds 5 business days.

Please check spelling, barcodes and dimensions carefully: once a proof is approved, errors in the approved artwork are not covered by our reprint policy.
