# Reprints, refunds and returns

Custom printed products cannot be returned for a change of mind, because they are made to order.

If your order arrives **damaged**, **misprinted** or **short** (fewer units than ordered), report it within **14 days of delivery** with photos and your order number. We then reprint the affected units for free or refund them.

Colour differences of up to 10% compared with your screen or a previous run are within normal print tolerance and are not a defect. Errors that were visible in the approved proof are not covered.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 business days after we approve the claim.
