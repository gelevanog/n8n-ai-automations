# Custom sizes and dielines

Every product is available in **custom dimensions** at no extra tooling cost for mailer boxes. Folding cartons in a new size need a one-off cutting die (EUR 150), which we keep for your reorders.

Enter your inner dimensions (length x width x height, in millimetres) in the configurator; we generate a dieline template for your artwork.

The maximum mailer box size is 600 x 400 x 300 mm. The smallest folding carton we make is 40 x 40 x 40 mm.
