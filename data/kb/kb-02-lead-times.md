# Production lead times

Standard production takes **10 business days** after you approve the digital proof. Large orders (above 10,000 units) take **15 business days**.

Shipping time is added on top of production (see "Shipping and delivery").

**Rush production** in **5 business days** is available for mailer boxes and stickers for orders up to 5,000 units, for a surcharge of 25% of the order value. Ask your account manager before ordering, as rush slots are limited each week.

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in the Netherlands, where our factory is located.
