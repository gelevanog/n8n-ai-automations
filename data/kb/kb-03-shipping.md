# Shipping and delivery

We ship from our factory in Utrecht, the Netherlands.

- **Netherlands, Belgium, Germany:** 1-2 business days, free for orders above EUR 500.
- **Rest of the EU:** 2-4 business days, flat rate EUR 45 per pallet or EUR 15 per parcel.
- **United Kingdom:** 3-5 business days, EUR 60 per pallet. UK import VAT and duties are paid by the recipient (DAP terms).
- **United States and Canada:** 7-10 business days by sea-air, quoted per order.

Every shipment gets a tracking link by email on the day it leaves the factory. We do not ship to PO boxes.
